Types of Games

Euro truck simulator 2

Euro Truck Simulator 2 is a realistic truck driving simulation game where players transport cargo across Europe, build and manage a trucking company, customize trucks, and explore detailed cities and highways while completing delivery missions..

Maleo bus simulator

Maleo Bus Simulator is a realistic bus driving game that lets you experience the daily life of a bus driver. You navigate city streets, pick up passengers, and follow traffic rules while enjoying detailed graphics and immersive gameplay. It’s great for anyone who loves simulation games and wants to try driving a bus virtually.

PES NBC game 2026

Games where players assume the roles of characters in a fictional world and develop their skills and storylines.

FTS NBC Game 2026

FTS NBC Game is a modified version of First Touch Soccer (FTS) that brings the excitement of Tanzania's NBC Premier League to mobile devices. It features updated teams, player transfers, official kits, club logos, realistic stadiums, and enhanced gameplay, giving fans an immersive local football experience.
